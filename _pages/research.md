### Vision-Language-Action models for robotics

<img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/research-vla.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="Vision and language inputs driving a VLA model that controls a robot arm" style="width:100%; max-width:760px; display:block; margin:1rem auto 1.25rem; border-radius:12px;">

I adapt and evaluate robot foundation models on real manipulation platforms. My focus is on fine-tuning strategies, evaluation metrics that go beyond task success rate, and making policies more reliable at deployment. This is my current work at KDDI Research.

---

### Multimodal affective computing

<img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/research-affective.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="Video, audio, physiological and text signals fused to predict emotion, engagement and personality" style="width:100%; max-width:760px; display:block; margin:1rem auto 1.25rem; border-radius:12px;">

I model human emotion, engagement, and personality from audio, video, text, and physiological signals such as EEG and ECG. This line of work, developed during my Ph.D. at IIT Roorkee, has appeared at ICML, ACM Multimedia, ICPR, FG, and IJCNN, and in Elsevier BSPC.
