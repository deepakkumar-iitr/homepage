Deepak Kumar

Postdoctoral Researcher, AI Division, KDDI Research, Inc.

KDDI Research, Inc.

Fujimino, Saitama, Japan

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the AI Division at KDDI Research, Inc., Japan, where I work on Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models for robot manipulation.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from IIT Roorkee, advised by Prof. Balasubramanian Raman, with a thesis on multimodal affect and behavior analysis. Before that, I was a research intern at Samsung Research Institute Bangalore, working on Vision-Language Models, and an Assistant Professor at GBPUAT Pantnagar.

My research interests include robot learning with VLA models, multimodal affective computing (audio, video, text, and physiological signals), and Vision-Language Models.
